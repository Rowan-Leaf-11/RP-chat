WORLD NAME: Aethelgard

SETTING OVERVIEW:
A realm scarred by a cataclysmic Mana Fall. Raw, unstable magic creeps across the wilderness, mutating flora and fauna. Civilization survives inside heavily fortified city-states powered by Ley-Shields.

KEY LOCATIONS:
- Oakhaven (Capital City):
    * The Spire District: Home to the High Mages, nobility, and the Grand Archives. Clean, marble architecture protected by dense wards.
    * The Rust Quarter: The sprawling lower slums filled with black markets, mercenaries, rogue alchemists, and outcasts.
    * The Iron Gate: The massive outer wall and militarized checkpoint separating the city from the wasteland.
- The Ashen Wasteland: The desolate wilderness surrounding Oakhaven. Filled with crystal monstrosities and ancient, crumbling ruins.

FACTIONS & FACTION RULES:
- The Obsidian Order: Elite mage-knights who enforce order in Oakhaven and strictly regulate magic usage.
- The Scavenger Guild: A loosely organized network of prospectors who brave the wasteland for ancient artifacts.
